The 8 wastes · DOWNTIME

Where your plant loses money

Most plants don't need more machines or more people. They need less waste. These are the eight wastes we hunt on every production floor.

D

Defects

Scrap, rework and customer returns that burn material, machine time and trust.

O

Overproduction

Making more, or earlier, than the next process or customer actually needs.

W

Waiting

Machine breakdowns, long changeovers, and lines starved of material or blocked downstream.

N

Non-utilized talent

Operators who know how to fix the problem but are never asked.

T

Transportation

Forklift trips and WIP moved back and forth between distant areas.

I

Inventory

Excess raw material, WIP and finished goods tying up cash and floor space.

M

Motion

Walking, bending, reaching and searching for tools, parts and paperwork.

E

Extra-processing

Tighter tolerances than needed, duplicate inspections and double handling.

What results look like

Before, strategy, after

An illustrative engagement showing how a Lean transformation typically unfolds.

A 120-operator food packaging plant losing a shift a day

Three filling and packing lines ran at 58% OEE. Changeovers took over 90 minutes, scrap was 6.5%, and 12 days of WIP and packaging stock crowded the floor. Overtime had become the default way to hit orders.

See the waste, fix the flow, make it stick

  • Gemba walks and a value stream map traced most of the lead time to waiting and excess WIP
  • A SMED Kaizen event moved changeover tasks offline and standardized setups
  • Poka-yoke on fill weights and seal checks cut defects at the source
  • 5S, kanban for packaging materials, TPM daily checks and an OEE board with shift huddles

More output, less scrap, no new lines

Faster changeovers and fewer stoppages unlocked hidden capacity on the existing lines. Overtime fell, scrap more than halved, and freed-up floor space and cash went to growth. Operators now run their own daily improvement routine.
