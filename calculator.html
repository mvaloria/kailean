How we calculate this

Labor waste: each waste has a benchmark share of operator time it typically consumes (e.g. waiting 7%, motion 4%), scaled by your rating (low ×0.5, medium ×1, high ×1.5). The combined total is capped at 30%.
labor waste = workers × cost per worker × labor waste %

Defects: material spend × scrap & rework rate, plus the rework labor above.

Inventory: carrying cost (storage, capital, handling, obsolescence) of about 25% per year, applied to the excess portion of your stock (15% / 30% / 45% for low / medium / high).
carrying cost = inventory × excess share × 25%

Hidden capacity: Lean typically lifts OEE by 5 / 10 / 15 points depending on how severe waiting and downtime are (capped at 85%, world class).
capacity gain = target OEE ÷ current OEE − 1

These are indicative estimates from common Lean manufacturing benchmarks. A diagnostic engagement replaces them with your plant's real data.
